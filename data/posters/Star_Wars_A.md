# Star Wars Style-A 
This post will only deal with the original US 1 sheet Style A poster. 
There were 4 printings of the Style A back in 1977.  The image is identical in all of them.  The only difference is the text in the bottom right corner.  The one that has the NSS number 77/21-0 is considered to be the first print.  The rest are missing the “-0” part.  Some prints also have the title underneath in “funky” font.  The 4th print is missing the “ONE SHEET- STYLE 'A'” text.


## Commercial Style-A Posters
There is also a 1993 version released by the German company ZigZag. The colors seem a little off on this print and it has a very obvious tell - It says on the bottom under the credits "©1993 Lucasfilm Ltd... Published by ZigZag Posters Germany ... ".

## Bootleg Style-A Posters
The most infamous bootleg of the Star Wars Style-A poster is the “hairy belt” Luke bootleg of the 1st print which has a hair on Luke's belt. 

## Rolled and Folded Style-A Posters
Almost all Style A posters were issued folded, very few exist rolled.  Rolled posters command a premium but it is unlikely you will find one in the wild.  A rolled poster usually means a fake or a commercial poster.
When a rolled Star Wars Stlye-A poster is found rolled, it is almost always a first print.  Second and Third print rolled examples are extremely rare.  As of this writing, a rolled 4th print is not known to exist.
